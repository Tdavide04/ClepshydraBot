# Clepshydra

The Clepshydra MTG Arena community: its members, the homebrew Artisan format it plays, and the Swiss
tournaments it runs. Terms are in English, as in the code; the Italian word members see in Discord is given
in parentheses.

## Community

**Member**:
A person in the Clepshydra Discord server.
_Avoid_: user (in prose about the community), account

**Presentation** (presentazione):
The introduction a new member submits through `/presentati`, published in the presentation channel.
_Avoid_: profile, verification

**Onboarding**:
The path from joining the server as a Viandante to becoming a Planeswalker by submitting a presentation.

**Viandante**:
The role of a member who joined but hasn't submitted a presentation yet.

**Planeswalker**:
The role of a verified member, granted when their presentation is published.

**Staff**:
The members allowed to run admin commands.
_Avoid_: admin (as a noun for people), moderator

**Log channel**:
The staff-only channel where the bot reports what it did and what went wrong.

**Community channel**:
The public channel where the bot posts announcements meant for every member.
_Avoid_: public log, announcements channel

## Artisan Format

**Artisan**:
The community's homebrew constructed format: MTG Arena cards printed at common or uncommon only, minus the banlist.
_Avoid_: pauper (a different, official format)

**Banlist**:
The cards banned in Artisan despite having a legal rarity, curated by staff.
_Avoid_: ban list (as an official Wizards list), B&R

**Deck** (mazzo):
A named list of cards a player brings, split into mainboard and sideboard.
_Avoid_: list, build

**Mainboard**:
The cards a deck plays with; at least 60.

**Sideboard**:
The optional extra cards of a deck; at most 15.

**Legal card**:
A card allowed in Artisan: not banned, and with at least one common or uncommon printing on Arena.
_Avoid_: valid card

**Rarity override**:
A card the community treats as common or uncommon even though Arena only prints it at a higher rarity, such as the Special Guests.

## Tournaments

**Tournament** (torneo):
A Swiss event with a fixed number of rounds, run on Discord through the bot.
_Avoid_: event (Wizards events are something else)

**Registration** (iscrizione):
A player's entry in a tournament, with or without a submitted deck.
_Avoid_: signup, entry

**Registration phase**:
The tournament status in which players can register, submit or change their deck, and leave.

**Leave**:
A player withdrawing their own registration during the registration phase (`/left_torneo`).

**Drop**:
Staff removing a player from a tournament (`/drop_giocatore`); after the start, the dropped player's pending match is lost.
_Avoid_: kick, disqualification

**Dropped player**:
A registration that has left or been dropped; it keeps its seed and, if it played, its place in the standings.

**Round** (turno):
One pass of pairings in which every active player plays at most one match.

**Pairing** (abbinamento):
The assignment of two players, or one player and a bye, to a table for a round.

**Bye**:
A round without an opponent, worth a win.

**Match** (partita):
Two players' best-of-three for one round, at one table.
_Avoid_: game (a single game inside a match)

**Result** (risultato):
The outcome of a match: win, loss or draw, with the games won by each player.

**Standings** (classifica):
The tournament ranking by points, then OMW, GWP, OGW and name.
_Avoid_: leaderboard (that is the rating ranking)

**Seed**:
A registration's fixed number in the tournament, used as a tiebreaker for pairings.

**OMW / GWP / OGW**:
Opponents' match-win %, game-win % and opponents' game-win %: the standings tiebreakers.

**Rating**:
A player's Glicko-2 score across all tournaments.
_Avoid_: ELO, MMR

**Leaderboard**:
The ranking of players by rating, across tournaments.

## Wizards Monitors

**Event Schedule**:
The Wizards page listing an Arena expansion's events by category, one page per set.

**Banned and Restricted Announcement**:
Wizards' periodic post announcing ban changes in the official formats; never applied to the Artisan banlist.
